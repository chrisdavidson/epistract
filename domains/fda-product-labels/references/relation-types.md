# Domain Relation Types

## HAS_ACTIVE_INGREDIENT
**DRUG_PRODUCT → ACTIVE_INGREDIENT**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to its ACTIVE_INGREDIENT(s)

## HAS_INACTIVE_INGREDIENT
**DRUG_PRODUCT → INACTIVE_INGREDIENT**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to its INACTIVE_INGREDIENT(s)

## MANUFACTURES
**MANUFACTURER → DRUG_PRODUCT**

A MANUFACTURER manufactures a DRUG_PRODUCT.

## INDICATED_FOR
**DRUG_PRODUCT → INDICATION**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to an INDICATION (FDA-approved use)

## CONTRAINDICATED_IN
**DRUG_PRODUCT → CONTRAINDICATION**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a CONTRAINDICATION (prohibited patient condition)

## CAUSES_ADVERSE_REACTION
**DRUG_PRODUCT → ADVERSE_REACTION**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to an ADVERSE_REACTION observed in clinical or post-marketing data

## HAS_WARNING
**DRUG_PRODUCT → WARNING**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a WARNING (boxed or standard precaution)

## INTERACTS_WITH
**DRUG_PRODUCT → DRUG_INTERACTION/DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to another DRUG_PRODUCT or substance involved in a clinically significant DRUG_INTERACTION

## DOSED_VIA
**DRUG_PRODUCT → DOSAGE_REGIMEN**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a DOSAGE_REGIMEN specifying how and when to administer it

## STUDIED_IN_POPULATION
**DRUG_PRODUCT → PATIENT_POPULATION**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a PATIENT_POPULATION with special safety or dosing notes

## HAS_MECHANISM
**DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT → MECHANISM_OF_ACTION**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to its MECHANISM_OF_ACTION

## HAS_PK_PROPERTY
**DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT → PHARMACOKINETIC_PROPERTY**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a PHARMACOKINETIC_PROPERTY

## EVALUATED_IN
**DRUG_PRODUCT → CLINICAL_STUDY**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to a CLINICAL_STUDY that supported label claims

## BELONGS_TO_CLASS
**DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT → PHARMACOLOGIC_CLASS**

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to its PHARMACOLOGIC_CLASS

## IDENTIFIES
**REGULATORY_IDENTIFIER → DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT**

A REGULATORY_IDENTIFIER identifies a DRUG_PRODUCT (NDC, NDA/ANDA, RxCUI, SPL set ID) or an ACTIVE_INGREDIENT (UNII).

## CONTRAINDICATED_WITH
**DRUG_PRODUCT → DRUG_PRODUCT/ACTIVE_INGREDIENT** (symmetric)

Links a DRUG_PRODUCT to another DRUG_PRODUCT whose co-administration is explicitly prohibited

## HAS_LABTEST
**DRUG_PRODUCT/WARNING → LABTEST**

The label calls for monitoring with a LABTEST — from the DRUG_PRODUCT, or from the WARNING the monitoring guards against.

## WARNS_OF
**WARNING → ADVERSE_REACTION**

A WARNING concerns a specific ADVERSE_REACTION (e.g. the boxed warning "Risk of thyroid C-cell tumors" warns of medullary thyroid carcinoma).

## INVOLVES
**DRUG_INTERACTION → ACTIVE_INGREDIENT/DRUG_PRODUCT**

A DRUG_INTERACTION involves an interacting drug or drug class — another DRUG_PRODUCT, or an ACTIVE_INGREDIENT such as "clarithromycin" or "strong CYP3A4 inhibitors".

## INCREASES_RISK_OF
**DRUG_INTERACTION → ADVERSE_REACTION**

A DRUG_INTERACTION increases the risk of an ADVERSE_REACTION (e.g. "Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors" interaction increases the risk of rhabdomyolysis).

## SPECIFIC_TO
**WARNING/DOSAGE_REGIMEN/CONTRAINDICATION/ADVERSE_REACTION → PATIENT_POPULATION**

A WARNING, DOSAGE_REGIMEN, CONTRAINDICATION, or ADVERSE_REACTION applies specifically to, or is elevated in, a PATIENT_POPULATION (e.g. the pediatric dosage regimen applies to pediatric patients; hepatosplenic T-cell lymphoma risk is elevated in young males with IBD).
